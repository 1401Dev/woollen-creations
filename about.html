About

A tiny studio, quietly at work.

Woollen Creations is a small home studio that makes things slowly. Everything you see on the site is worked by hand — crocheted, knit or wet-felted — from natural fibres that feel honest against the skin.

We started because a friend asked for a tiny bear. One bear became a pair, then a family, and soon there were cats and scarves and mushrooms and cushions, and a little row of yarn baskets along the wall. The studio keeps that spirit: pieces are made one at a time, and no two are quite the same.

We work in small batches so each piece gets the time it needs. A cable scarf takes about a day of focused work; an amigurumi can take two. Nothing is machine-finished, and nothing is rushed.

Where

Made in a sunlit back room, next to a very opinionated cat.

What

Natural wool, cotton thread, wooden buttons, nothing plastic.

When

New pieces appear every couple of weeks.

Commissions

Have something in mind? Most pieces in the gallery can be remade in different colours, and I take on a small number of custom commissions each month. Get in touch with a rough idea and I'll come back with a sketch and a timeline.
